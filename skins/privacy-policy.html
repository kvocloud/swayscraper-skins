Swayscraper: Learn English – Privacy Policy

Developer: KV Group · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Swayscraper: Learn English (package com.kvgroup.towerstacker) handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (best scores, hearts, owned skins, city progress, learned words, sound and language settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Downloading skin packs

The app may download additional skin packs (images and word lists) from a public file server (GitHub). These requests do not contain any personal information or identifiers. Like any web server, the hosting provider may receive your device's IP address as part of the connection; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Text-to-speech

Words are read aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

4. Advertising

The current version contains no advertising and no analytics. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children

The app is designed for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Permissions

The only permission used is Internet access, to download skin packs. No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions are requested.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: kvgroup68@gmail.com

Chính sách quyền riêng tư

Nhà phát triển: KV Group · Cập nhật: 10/11/2026
